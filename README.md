# Privacy Policy — Mandal Manager

**Last updated:** [05 -08-2026]

This policy explains what information Mandal Manager ("the App") handles, how it's stored, and what it doesn't do. It's written in plain language on purpose, describing what the App actually does rather than asserting compliance with any specific law — see the Disclaimer section at the end for why that distinction matters.

---

## 1. Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to anyone using Mandal Manager — whether set up as a President/Treasurer device or a Coordinator device — for managing a community festival group's donations, expenses, tasks, contacts, and related records.

---

## 2. The Short Version

Mandal Manager stores everything **directly on your device**. There is no company server, no cloud database, and no backend the App reports to. Nothing you enter — a donor's name, a phone number, a rupee amount, a photo, an uploaded file — is transmitted anywhere by the App itself. The only way any of that information leaves your device is if you, the user, deliberately choose to send it somewhere yourself (for example, tapping "Share" to send a receipt over WhatsApp). That is your own action, not something the App does on its own.

---

## 3. What Information the App Stores (Locally, On Your Device Only)

Depending on how you use it, the App may store:

- **Donation records** — donor names, donation amounts, dates and times, payment mode (cash or UPI), and a UPI transaction reference number if you choose to enter one
- **Donor mobile numbers**, if you choose to add them, used only to address WhatsApp/SMS messages directly and to enable the receipt-sharing features
- **Expense records** — vendor/item names, amounts, dates, and notes
- **Task and contact information** you enter, including names and phone numbers for people in your organization
- **The Coordinator/President profile** you set up on that specific device — a name, phone number, and, if you choose to set one, a security PIN
- **A Mandal name and logo image**, if you upload one, used to personalize receipts and reports
- **Audio files (MP3s)**, if you choose to upload any, for offline playback
- **PDF file attachments**, if you choose to attach one to an entry — for example, a vendor's receipt or a scanned copy of a permission document you've already obtained from the relevant local authority (see Section 8 for an important clarification about this)
- **Backup files** you create yourself, in a `.mmr` format specific to this App, which you control and choose where to save or send

All of the above lives in your device's own local storage. None of it is copied to any server operated by the App, because no such server exists.

---

## 4. What Happens When You Choose to Share Something

The App includes features to share a donation receipt or a report via WhatsApp, SMS, or as a downloadable/shareable PDF file. When you use these features:

- You are the one choosing to send the information, and you are choosing who it goes to (either by picking a contact yourself, or, if a donor's number is saved, the App pre-addresses the message to that number for your convenience)
- Once a message or file leaves your device through WhatsApp, SMS, or your phone's own share menu, it is subject to **that other app's own privacy practices**, not this App's — we have no visibility into or control over what happens to information after it's handed off to WhatsApp, your SMS provider, or whatever app you choose to share a PDF through

---

## 5. Advertising

This App may display banner advertisements through Google AdMob to support its continued availability at no cost. AdMob may collect certain information for the purpose of showing and measuring ads — this is governed by Google's own privacy policy and AdMob's own data practices, not by this App directly, since we do not control what AdMob itself collects for ad-serving purposes. You can review Google's practices at their own published privacy policy.

---

## 6. Permissions This App May Request

- **Contacts** — used only if you choose to add a contact card to your phone's own contacts directory, or import an existing phone contact into the App. The App does not read or transmit your contacts for any other purpose.
- **Notifications** — used only to remind you about a task you've set a reminder for.
- **Storage (Files/Documents)** — used only to save the reports, exports, and backups you generate, to your own device's storage.

None of these permissions are used to collect information for any purpose beyond the specific feature they support.

---

## 7. Data You Control

Because everything is stored on your own device:

- **Deleting the App, or clearing its data through your device settings, permanently removes everything the App has stored.** There is no copy anywhere else to recover, because none exists.
- You can export a full backup of your own data at any time, and choose exactly what to include.
- Nothing about your usage is visible to us, because there is no mechanism by which it could be — we do not operate any system that receives data from installed copies of this App.

---

## 8. About Documents You Attach (Vendor Receipts, Permission Letters, and Similar Files)

The App allows attaching a single PDF file (up to 2MB) to certain entries — for example, a vendor's bill, or a copy of a permission or permit you have obtained from a local authority for your event. This feature exists purely as **a place for you to keep your own copy of a document you already possess**, for your own organizational and record-keeping purposes.

To be clear about what this feature does and does not do:

- The App does not issue, verify, validate, apply for, or have any relationship with any government body, licensing authority, or religious institution.
- The App does not check whether an attached document is genuine, current, complete, or sufficient for any legal or administrative purpose.
- Storing a document in the App has no bearing on your actual legal obligations to obtain any required permits, licenses, or permissions from the appropriate local authorities for your event — those obligations exist independently of this App and are entirely between you and the relevant authority.

## 9. Religious and Cultural Neutrality

Mandal Manager was originally built with Ganesh festival community groups in mind, and some of its terminology (such as "Aarti") reflects that origin. The App itself is a general-purpose organizational and record-keeping tool for community festival groups broadly — including but not limited to Ganesh Mandals, Navratri committees, and groups organizing observances for other occasions. The App does not represent, endorse, or take a position on behalf of any religious or cultural practice; it simply provides the same organizational features (donation tracking, task management, contact records) regardless of the occasion a group is using it for.

This App is intended for use by adult members of community organizations managing festival logistics and finances, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children through this App.
